\mychapter{}  \myverse*{}The elder, to the chosen lady and her children, whom I love in truth, and not I only, but also all those who know the truth, \myverse{}for the truth’s sake, which remains in us, and it will be with us forever: \myverse{}Grace, mercy, and peace will be with us, from God the Father and from the Lord Yeshua the Messiah,\footnote{ 2 Yochanan 1:3  “Messiah” means “Anointed One”.} the Son of the Father, in truth and love.


\myverse{}I rejoice greatly that I have found some of your children walking in truth, even as we have been commanded by the Father. \myverse{}Now I beg you, dear lady, not as though I wrote to you a new commandment, but that which we had from the beginning, that we love one another. \myverse{}This is love, that we should walk according to his commandments. This is the commandment, even as you heard from the beginning, that you should walk in it.


\myverse{}For many deceivers have gone out into the world, those who don’t confess that Yeshua the Messiah came in the flesh. This is the deceiver and the Anti-messiah. \myverse{}Watch yourselves, that we don’t lose the things which we have accomplished, but that we receive a full reward. \myverse{}Whoever transgresses and doesn’t remain in the teaching of Messiah doesn’t have God. He who remains in the teaching has both the Father and the Son. \myverse{}If anyone comes to you and doesn’t bring this teaching, don’t receive him into your house, and don’t welcome him, \myverse{}for he who welcomes him participates in his evil deeds.


\myverse{}Having many things to write to you, I don’t want to do so with paper and ink, but I hope to come to you and to speak face to face, that our joy may be made full. \myverse{}The children of your chosen sister greet you. Amen.
