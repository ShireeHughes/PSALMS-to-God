\mybook{2 Timothy}
\vspace{-1.36in}


\mychapter{} \myverse*{}Paul, an emissary of Yeshua the Messiah\footnote{1:1 “Messiah” means “Anointed One”.} through the will of God, according to the promise of
the life which is in Messiah Yeshua, \myverse{}to Timothy, my beloved child: Grace, mercy, and peace, from God the Father and Messiah Yeshua our Lord.


\myverse{}I thank God, whom I serve as my forefathers did, with a pure conscience. How unceasing is my memory of you in my petitions, night and day \myverse{}longing to see you, remembering your tears, that I may be filled with joy; \myverse{}having been reminded of the sincere faith that is in you, which lived first in your grandmother Lois and your mother Eunice and, I am persuaded, in you also.


\myverse{}For this cause, I remind you that you should stir up the gift of God which is in you through the laying on of my hands. \myverse{}For God didn’t give us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control. \myverse{}Therefore don’t be ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner; but endure hardship for the Good News according to the power of God, \myverse{}who saved us and called us with a holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given to us in Messiah Yeshua before times eternal, \myverse{}but has now been revealed by the appearing of our Savior, Messiah Yeshua, who abolished death, and brought life and immortality to light through the Good News. \myverse{}For this I was appointed as a proclaimer, an emissary, and a teacher of the Gentiles. \myverse{}For this cause I also suffer these things.

Yet I am not ashamed, for I know him whom I have believed, and I am persuaded that he is able to guard that which I have committed to him against that day.


\myverse{}Hold the pattern of sound words which you have heard from me, in faith and love which is in Messiah Yeshua. \myverse{}That good thing which was committed to you, guard through the Holy Spirit who dwells in us.


\myverse{}This you know, that all who are in Asia turned away from me, of whom are Phygelus and Hermogenes. \myverse{}May the Lord grant mercy to the house of Onesiphorus, for he often refreshed me, and was not ashamed of my chain, \myverse{}but when he was in Rome, he sought me diligently and found me \myverse{}(the Lord grant to him to find the Lord’s mercy in that day); and in how many things he served at Ephesus, you know very well.


% 2 TIMOTHY 2
\mychapter{} \myverse*{}You therefore, my child, be strengthened in the grace that is in Messiah Yeshua. \myverse{}The things which you have heard from me among many witnesses, commit the same things to faithful men who will be able to teach others also. \myverse{}You therefore must endure hardship as a good soldier of Messiah Yeshua. \myverse{}No soldier on duty entangles himself in the affairs of life, that he may please him who enrolled him as a soldier. \myverse{}Also, if anyone competes in athletics, he isn’t crowned unless he has competed by the rules. \myverse{}The farmer who labors must be the first to get a share of the crops. \myverse{}Consider what I say, and may the Lord give you understanding in all things.


\myverse{}Remember Yeshua the Messiah, risen from the dead, of the offspring\footnote{2:8 or, seed} of David, according to my Good News, \myverse{}in which I suffer hardship to the point of chains as a criminal. But God’s word isn’t chained. \myverse{}Therefore I endure all things for the chosen ones’ sake, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Messiah Yeshua with eternal glory. \myverse{}This saying is trustworthy:

“For if we died with him,

we will also live with him.


\myverse{}If we endure,

we will also reign with him.

If we deny him,

he also will deny us.


\myverse{}If we are faithless,

he remains faithful;

for he can’t deny himself.”


\myverse{}Remind them of these things, charging them in the sight of the Lord that they don’t argue about words to no profit, to the subverting of those who hear.


\myverse{}Give diligence to present yourself approved by God, a workman who doesn’t need to be ashamed, properly handling the Word of Truth. \myverse{}But shun empty chatter, for it will go further in ungodliness, \myverse{}and those words will consume like gangrene, of whom is Hymenaeus and Philetus: \myverse{}men who have erred concerning the truth, saying that the resurrection is already past, and overthrowing the faith of some. \myverse{}However, God’s firm foundation stands, having this seal: “The Lord knows those who are his,”\footnote{2:19 Numbers 16:5} and, “Let every one who names the name of the Lord\footnote{2:19 TR reads “Messiah” instead of “the Lord”} depart from unrighteousness.”


\myverse{}Now in a large house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of clay. Some are for honor and some for dishonor. \myverse{}If anyone therefore purges himself from these, he will be a vessel for honor, sanctified, and suitable for the master’s use, prepared for every good work.


\myverse{}Flee from youthful lusts; but pursue righteousness, faith, love, and peace with those who call on the Lord out of a pure heart. \myverse{}But refuse foolish and ignorant questionings, knowing that they generate strife. \myverse{}The Lord’s servant must not quarrel, but be gentle toward all, able to teach, patient, \myverse{}in gentleness correcting those who oppose him. Perhaps God may give them repentance leading to a full knowledge of the truth, \myverse{}and they may recover themselves out of the devil’s snare, having been taken captive by him to do his will.


% 2 TIMOTHY 3
\mychapter{} \myverse*{}But know this: that in the last days, grievous times will come. \myverse{}For men will be lovers of self, lovers of money, boastful, arrogant, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy, \myverse{}without natural affection, unforgiving, slanderers, without self-control, fierce, not lovers of good, \myverse{}traitors, headstrong, conceited, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, \myverse{}holding a form of godliness but having denied its power. Turn away from these, also. \myverse{}For some of these are people who creep into houses and take captive gullible women loaded down with sins, led away by various lusts, \myverse{}always learning and never able to come to the knowledge of the truth. \myverse{}Even as Jannes and Jambres opposed Moses, so these also oppose the truth, men corrupted in mind, who concerning the faith are rejected. \myverse{}But they will proceed no further. For their folly will be evident to all men,
as theirs also came to be.


\myverse{}But you followed my teaching, conduct, purpose, faith, patience, love, steadfastness, \myverse{}persecutions, and sufferings—those things that happened to me at Antioch, Iconium, and Lystra. I endured those persecutions. The Lord delivered me out of them all. \myverse{}Yes, and all who desire to live godly in Messiah Yeshua will suffer persecution. \myverse{}But evil men and impostors will grow worse and worse, deceiving and being deceived. \myverse{}But you remain in the things which you have learned and have been assured of, knowing from whom you have learned them. \myverse{}From infancy, you have known the holy Scriptures which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith which is in Messiah Yeshua. \myverse{}Every Scripture is God-breathed and\footnote{3:16 or, Every writing inspired by God is} profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for instruction in righteousness, \myverse{}that each person who belongs to God may be complete, thoroughly equipped for every good work.


%2 TIMONTHY 4
\mychapter{} \myverse*{}I command you therefore before God and the Lord Yeshua the Messiah, who will judge the living and the dead at his appearing and his Kingdom: \myverse{}proclaim the word; be urgent in season and out of season; reprove, rebuke, and exhort with all patience and teaching. \myverse{}For the time will come when they will not listen to the sound doctrine, but having itching ears, will heap up for themselves teachers after their own lusts, \myverse{}and will turn away their ears from the truth, and turn away to fables. \myverse{}But you be sober in all things, suffer hardship, do the work of an evangelist, and fulfill your ministry.


\myverse{}For I am already being offered, and the time of my departure has come. \myverse{}I have fought the good fight. I have finished the course. I have kept the faith. \myverse{}From now on, the crown of righteousness is stored up for me, which the Lord, the righteous judge, will give to me on that day; and not to me only, but also to all those who have loved his appearing.


\myverse{}Be diligent to come to me soon, \myverse{}for Demas left me, having loved this present world, and went to Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia; and Titus to Dalmatia. \myverse{}Only Luke is with me. Take Mark and bring him with you, for he is useful to me for service. \myverse{}But I sent Tychicus to Ephesus. \myverse{}Bring the cloak that I left at Troas with Carpus when you come—and the books, especially the parchments. \myverse{}Alexander the coppersmith did much evil to me. The Lord will repay him according to his deeds. \myverse{}Beware of him, for he greatly opposed our words.


\myverse{}At my first defense, no one came to help me, but all left me. May it not be held against them. \myverse{}But the Lord stood by me and strengthened me, that through me the message might be fully proclaimed, and that all the Gentiles might hear. So I was delivered out of the mouth of the lion. \myverse{}And the Lord will deliver me from every evil work and will preserve me for his heavenly Kingdom. To him be the glory forever and ever. Amen.


\myverse{}Greet Prisca and Aquila, and the house of Onesiphorus. \myverse{}Erastus remained at Corinth, but I left Trophimus at Miletus sick. \myverse{}Be diligent to come before winter. Eubulus salutes you, as do Pudens, Linus, Claudia, and all the brothers.


\myverse{}The Lord Yeshua the Messiah be with your spirit. Grace be with
you. Amen.
